Project Data, Coverage & Risk-Card Quality Overview

Descriptive statistics for the AI knowledge-ecosystem project: a single semantic space (the Science–Technology–Policy Interface, STPI) over Korean and international AI academic papers, patents, and policy / institutional documents (1990–2026), plus a global AI-risk overlay. Here the Science domain is read broadly as all AI-related research and therefore includes the AI-risk research literature; the Policy domain likewise includes AI-risk governance standards (NIST AI RMF, OECD, ISO, OWASP, MITRE, NHTSA). Document accounting: Total literature = Science + Patents + Policy + Risk materials, where Risk materials = risk research papers + reports + simple sources (plus risk-related policy reports). Risk records that are academic papers and not already present in Science are deduplicated and folded into Science (26,760 added, so Science = 34,664 + 26,760 = 61,424); 3,491 risk-related policy reports are reassigned from Policy to Risk materials. The 1990–2026 STPI reference space itself is built from the 111,384 paper+patent+policy documents, with the AI-risk literature projected onto it as an overlay. The AI-risk charts, evidence-status counts, risk-card quality audit, and seminal-reference list are computed from the published data.

Sources. Documents projected into the STPI space (m2 L3-activation manifest): papers 34,664; patents 67,727; policy 8,993 (total 111,384). The policy corpus was later expanded to 12,002 (v5 core) / 14,660 (v5 collection by period, charted above). Reference hierarchy: l0_l1_l2_l3_hierarchy_counts.csv, consolidated to 1,898 L3 cards after canonicalization, L2 rebalancing, and near-duplicate merging (L2 clusters: 80). AI-risk literature counts: risk-overlay descriptive statistics (26,937 broad / 17,685 affiliation-ready records). AI-risk node charts are computed at load time from data/global_ai_risk_l4_overlay_nodes.json, consolidated to 1,699 L4 risk cards after duplicate-card merging. Korean policy evidence has been attached to 518 L4 risk cards by matching 949 domestic AI-risk policy candidates to risk-card definitions with BGE-M3 cosine similarity (threshold 0.56); the match table is stored in data/korean_policy_ai_risk_l4_matches.csv. The risk-card quality audit is stored in data/risk_card_quality_audit_summary.json; it separates cards already backed by direct evidence from cards still queued for source-link refinement or evidence-status review. The Science domain is presented inclusive of AI-risk research (26,937 papers) and Policy inclusive of AI-risk governance standards. Seminal top-venue references are stored in data/key_papers.json — 39 papers ranked by relevance / importance / originality and linked to 125 L4 nodes. The duplicate-card audit is stored in data/taxonomy_card_deduplication_audit.csv. Reference-taxonomy refinement (2026-06): L3 labels are canonicalized (Title-case, acronym-preserving) with cross-domain concepts linked by shared canonical IDs; the degenerate single-child L1 level was removed (now Domain→L2→L3); each L3 is tagged by node type (method, task, concept, principle, instrument, risk, field); oversized L2 clusters were rebalanced (max cluster 90→46); and 2,749 nodes were mapped to authoritative sources (ISO/IEC 22989, NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act, MIT AI Risk Repository, OECD, and standard texts) where defined.
